V3 CUSTOM FUNCTIONS 0-9
========================

10 separate custom digit functions for V3.
Each digit is a single digit plane that moves from the FRONT face toward the BACK face.

Coordinate convention:
X = left/right
Y = front -> back
    Y=0 = FRONT
    Y=7 = BACK
Z = bottom/top
F = animation frame

Motion:
Y == (F % 8)
Therefore the digit starts at the FRONT (Y=0) and moves one layer at a time toward the BACK (Y=7), then repeats.

IMPORTANT: These are the exact function expressions to copy one at a time.
The current V3 function parser supports X,Y,Z,F, %, comparisons, && and ||.

CUSTOM 0
@((Y==(F%8))&&((((X==2)||(X==5))&&((Z>=1)&&(Z<=6)))||(((Z==1)||(Z==6))&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))))

CUSTOM 1
@((Y==(F%8))&&(((X==4)&&(Z>=1)&&(Z<=6))||((Z==1)&&(X>=3)&&(X<=5))))

CUSTOM 2
@((Y==(F%8))&&((((Z==6)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((Z==4)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((Z==1)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((X==5)&&((Z>=4)&&(Z<=6)))||((X==2)&&((Z>=1)&&(Z<=4))))))

CUSTOM 3
@((Y==(F%8))&&((((Z==6)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((Z==4)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((Z==1)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((X==5)&&((Z>=1)&&(Z<=6))))))

CUSTOM 4
@((Y==(F%8))&&((((X==2)&&((Z>=4)&&(Z<=6)))||((X==5)&&((Z>=1)&&(Z<=6)))||((Z==4)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5))))))

CUSTOM 5
@((Y==(F%8))&&((((Z==6)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((Z==4)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((Z==1)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((X==2)&&((Z>=4)&&(Z<=6)))||((X==5)&&((Z>=1)&&(Z<=4))))))

CUSTOM 6

CUSTOM 7
@((Y==(F%8))&&((((Z==6)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((X==5)&&((Z>=1)&&(Z<=6))))))

CUSTOM 8
@((Y==(F%8))&&((((Z==6)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((Z==4)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((Z==1)&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((X==2)&&((Z>=1)&&(Z<=6)))||((X==5)&&((Z>=1)&&(Z<=6))))))

CUSTOM 9
@((Y==(F%8))&&((X==5)||(((((Z==6)||(Z==4))||(Z==1))&&((X>=2)&&(X<=5)))||((X==2)&&(Z>=4)))))

Verified design intent:
- At F=0, digit is on Y=0 FRONT.
- At F=1, digit is on Y=1.
- ...
- At F=7, digit is on Y=7 BACK.
- At F=8, motion repeats from Y=0 FRONT.
- CUSTOM 9 is compressed to stay within the V3 parser bytecode capacity while preserving the 9 shape.
